description=[h2]🚀 Quick Start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Get empty capsules and Pal Spheres: loot mechanic shops, car supply stores, barns, pet stores and hunting lockers, or get them from an admin or the Economy Center; when the server turns crafting on, you can also make them from vanilla parts at a table
description=[*] Park, turn the engine off and make sure nobody is inside, then right-click the vehicle and choose [b]Pack into Capsule[/b]; right-click a live animal, open its submenu and choose [b]Catch in Pal Sphere[/b]; or right-click an empty capsule or Pal Sphere in your inventory and choose [b]Throw to Capture[/b]
description=[*] For a vehicle, right-click the capsule, choose [b]Throw Capsule[/b], pick a landing spot with the cursor and left-click; too heavy to pick up? Right-click it on the ground and choose [b]Press the Button[/b]
description=[*] For an animal, right-click the Pal Sphere holding it in your inventory and choose [b]Release[/b]; the animal appears right next to you
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Pack vehicles in capsules[/b]: right-click a vehicle to pack it; seven capsule types, each with its own smoke and sounds
description=[*] [b]Catch animals in Pal Spheres[/b]: right-click an animal to catch it, and release it right next to you
description=[*] [b]Four Pal Sphere tiers[/b]: better tiers catch better, and the wild catch chance is shown before you try
description=[*] [b]Throw to capture[/b]: throw an empty capsule at a vehicle or an empty Pal Sphere at an animal, even a wild one that runs from you
description=[*] [b]Kept as they were[/b]: a vehicle's mods, condition and cargo, an animal's breed, age, genes and pregnancy
description=[*] [b]Tooltip and 3D preview[/b]: hover a capsule or Pal Sphere to see a vehicle's condition or an animal's details, and spin a 3D view of a vehicle
description=[*] [b]Safehouse protection[/b]: vehicles and animals in someone else's safehouse can't be packed, and nothing can be released there
description=[*] [b]No copies, nothing lost[/b]: a crash or a rolled-back save never creates an extra vehicle, animal or cargo, and admins can trace a lost capsule and restore what it held
description=[/list]
