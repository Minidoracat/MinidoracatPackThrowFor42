    /* Crafting empty capsules and Pal Spheres: three recipes per type, CraftMinidoracatPackThrow<empty item><Level>
       with Level Easy, Standard or Hard (Recipes.name). Sandbox Craft<id> picks the one level players get, or none (the
       default; user 2026-10-25, design packthrow-craft-1025), and a type switched off is never crafted (Rules.craftOn).
       OnTest and OnAddToMenu both name the global MinidoracatPackThrow_craft<id><Level> (lua/shared/MinidoracatPackThrow/
       Recipes.lua: OnTest resolves dotted names, OnAddToMenu only globals).
       - category MinidoracatPackThrow: one craft category of this mod in both crafting windows. Its name is
         IGUI_CraftingCategories_MinidoracatPackThrow (ISWidgetRecipeCategories.lua:100) and its Neat Crafting icon
         media/ui/CategoryIcon/MinidoracatPackThrow.png (NC_CategoryList_Slot.lua:32-44); the spelling must match.
       - module Base: short recipe names resolve in Base only (family pitfalls.md "CraftRecipe"); items keep their
         module and are written as full types. NeedToBeLearn stays false: no magazine to keep.
       - Laid out like the vanilla electrical recipes (recipes_radio.txt CraftMakeshiftRadio, recipes_electrical.txt
         MakeImprovisedFlashlight): a work surface (AnySurfaceCraft), MakingElectrical.
       - Standard is the recipe of 0.3.0. Easy: skills -2 (none below 1), half the time and XP (none for a skill no
         longer asked), no pliers, without the rarest part, half the counts (at least 1). Hard: skills +2, twice the
         time, 1.5 times the XP, one rare part more, twice the counts of scrap, wire and screws.
       - The seven capsules of one level take the same parts (only the looks differ): a car battery's power, any charge
         (drainable: mode:destroy with ItemCount, else empty ones are swept up with it; the same for the battery of a
         hard Pal Sphere), a shell and the electronics.
       - Pal Spheres climb like Palworld's: the Pal Sphere from parts, each higher tier from an empty one of the tier
         below plus parts, at every level.
       Names: Translate/<language>/Recipes.json, keyed by recipe name (Translator.getRecipeName). */
